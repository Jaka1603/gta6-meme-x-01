# gta6-meme-x-01 — Meme Candidates ROUND 2

Round 1 was rejected by Jaka: *"kurang bagus dan kurang mengekspresikan"*
(not good enough, not expressive enough). Round 2 is a ground-up redesign
around one rule: **the emotion must explode off the image in under a
second at the judge's 768×480 view.** Same competition facts as round 1
(see CANDIDATES.md): entry = the X post, first picture judged as a JPEG
fitted inside 768×480, criteria in order (1) make.fun recognisably IN the
picture, (2) funny, (3) GTA 6 specifically, (4) sells make.fun,
(5) short readable caption, (6) original, (7) clean image. Post text must
carry the bounty link and the play code **`makefun-sauce-peony-dipper`**
(NOT the referral code pse7dwj5). Field calibration unchanged: dev-board
#1 sits at 88.7%, winning pattern = concrete artifact/document formats,
the ghost as an actor, an agent-earns twist.

## What changed vs round 1 (honest notes)

- **Faces first.** Round 1 led with wide scenes (a skyline, a museum, a
  shop shelf) and small people. Every round-2 scene is an EXTREME
  close-up human face — screaming rage, jaw-dropped shock, uncontrollable
  sobbing, tears-of-joy laughter, smug grin, ecstatic celebration —
  drawn in GTA loading-screen illustration style, filling ≥60% of the
  frame. Backgrounds are reduced to a neon Vice City hint.
- **The ghost got a job.** Round 1's ghost mostly stood in scenes. Here
  it is composited LARGE (up to ~60% of frame height) with an emotional
  role: smugly collecting its 48th paid bounty next to a meltdown,
  celebrating in the "paid" panel, presenting a giant pension cheque,
  holding its star coin out to a sobbing gamer.
- **Contrast inside one image.** Split panels (crying/laughing),
  rage vs calm, shock vs earnings — the joke reads before any text.
- **Text got shorter and bigger.** Two lines max, Anton, white + magenta
  accent, verified legible on a judge-size contact sheet
  (`work/contact-r2-final.jpg`).
- **One declared re-execution:** R2-3 borrows round 1's pension axis
  (C5) but is a total redo — new scene (tears-of-joy close-up), new
  device (a giant make.fun cheque as the punchline). Flagged honestly:
  its headline leans on the "13 years" wait axis, which is crowded in
  the field; it is ranked 4th partly for that reason.
- All scenes are new AI generations with NO text in them; every letter
  is PIL-drawn (Anton/Archivo Black), same as round 1. All outputs are
  flattened JPEGs.
